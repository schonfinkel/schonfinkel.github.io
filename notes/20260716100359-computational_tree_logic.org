#+begin_quote
CTL has a two-stage syntax where formulae in CTL are classified into state and
path formulae. The former are assertions about the atomic propositions in the
states and their branching structure, while path formulae express temporal
properties of paths. Compared to [[id:4f82143b-7ad7-4020-9c65-1f4495e54405][LTL]] formulae, path formulae in CTL are simpler:
as in LTL they are built by the next-step and until operators, but they must not
be combined with Boolean connectives and no nesting of temporal modalities is
allowed.

[cite:@baier2008principles p.317 chap.6 part.6.2]
#+end_quote
